Services and fees

We offer general and family dentistry for patients of all ages. Our dentists will explain your options and the cost before any treatment starts, and you can ask for a written quote at any time.

General dentistry

Check-ups, cleans, fillings, root canal treatment and extractions. We see children from their first birthday and we are happy to see nervous patients at a slower pace.

Cosmetic dentistry

Whitening, veneers and tooth-coloured fillings. Ask us for a consultation and we will talk you through what is possible for your teeth.
